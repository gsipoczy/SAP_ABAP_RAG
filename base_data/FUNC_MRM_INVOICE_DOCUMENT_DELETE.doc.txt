&FUNCTIONALITY&
The invoice (I_BELNR, I_GJAHR) is deleted.
All data that was created during invoice verification is deleted. The
following records are deleted:
relevant record in RBKP
relevant record in RBKPB
relevant records in RBSELBEST/FRBR/LIFS/WERK
relevant records in RBVD
relevant records in RBVDMAT
relevant records in RBDRSEG
If the parameter I_LOCK is set, the invoice is protected with a logical
block during processing. If the invoice is blocked by another user, the
exception INVOICE_LOCKED is raised.
